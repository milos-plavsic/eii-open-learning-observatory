# Community code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and evidence-oriented community.
Participants must engage constructively, respect differing educational and
linguistic contexts, protect learner privacy, and avoid harassment,
discrimination, intimidation, or publication of another person's private
information.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation
when behavior threatens people, data, or the integrity of the project. Report
conduct concerns privately to `support@eii.edu.eu`. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as reasonably possible and reviewed without retaliation.

This code applies in repository spaces and when representing the project in
public or private project-related communication.
